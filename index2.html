Questions

Good to know before you join.

Is Weplace free to download?

Yes. You can download Weplace from the App Store or Google Play using the buttons on this page.

Can I start selling from home?

Yes. Register as a seller in the app, add your products, and start reaching nearby buyers. You can submit your verification documents from the Seller Hub.

How are sellers verified?

Through seller registration in the app, personal sellers can register with IC verification, while company sellers can provide SSM information and optional supporting documents such as halal certification.

Is it available in my area?

Weplace is open to communities across Malaysia. If your group does not exist yet, register and create your community in the app to be the first in your area.
