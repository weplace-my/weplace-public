HOW TO BE A SELLER

Start selling, your way.

Set up your shop, manage your products, and grow at a pace that works for you.

Register as a seller

Register in the app on a trusted platform. Personal sellers verify with a Malaysian IC, while companies register with Malaysian SSM details.

Shop profile

Set up your shop name, description, gallery, and validation details so buyers know who they are buying from.

Manage products

Add and update products, prices, availability, and photos from one place.

Manage selling

Set payment, delivery or pickup, selling area, options, and business hours. Sell when and how it works for you.

Manage orders

Track orders from preparing to delivering and completing, with clear updates at every step.

Trust for local participation in Malaysia.

Neighbourhood commerce works better when people know who they are dealing with. Weplace supports clearer seller verification and more familiar local interactions.

Personal Seller

Malaysian personal sellers can register by uploading IC front and back for verification.

Company Seller

Company sellers can register with SSM information, with halal certification as an optional supporting document.
